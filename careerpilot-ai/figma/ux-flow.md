# CareerPilot AI — UX Flow & Figma Handoff

## 1. Purpose

This document connects the CareerPilot AI product requirements with the UX flow and Figma design.

The goal is to ensure that product strategy, user stories, acceptance criteria and UX screens remain traceable throughout the product lifecycle.

---

## 2. End-to-End UX Flow

The primary user journey is:

**Assess → Analyze → Discover → Compare → Decide → Plan → Refine**

```text
Welcome
   ↓
Career Goal
   ↓
Professional Profile
   ↓
Skills & Experience
   ↓
Career Preferences & Priorities
   ↓
Assessment Summary
   ↓
Career Fit Analysis
   ↓
Career Options
   ↓
Compare Options
   ↓
Preferred Career Direction
   ↓
Initial Transition Plan
   ↓
Feedback & Refinement
