# CareerPilot AI — User Stories

## Epic 1 — Career Assessment & Profile

### US-01 — Enter Professional Experience

**As a** professional exploring a career transition,  
**I want to** provide my professional experience,  
**so that** CareerPilot AI can understand my career background.

### US-02 — Capture Current Role and Experience

**As a** user,  
**I want to** provide my current role, industry and years of experience,  
**so that** the product can evaluate my existing career context.

### US-03 — Edit Professional Profile

**As a** user,  
**I want to** review and edit my professional profile,  
**so that** I can keep my information accurate.

### US-04 — Add Skills and Experience

**As a** user,  
**I want to** add my technical, functional, domain and transferable skills,  
**so that** CareerPilot AI can understand my capabilities.

### US-05 — Define Skill Proficiency

**As a** user,  
**I want to** indicate my proficiency level for each skill,  
**so that** the product can consider the depth of my experience.

### US-06 — Identify Transferable Skills

**As a** user,  
**I want CareerPilot AI to** identify transferable skills from my experience,  
**so that** I can understand capabilities that may apply to other career paths.

### US-07 — Define Career Goals and Priorities

**As a** user,  
**I want to** define my career goals and priorities,  
**so that** recommendations reflect what matters most to me.

### US-08 — Define Preferred Work Style

**As a** user,  
**I want to** define my preferred work style and work environment,  
**so that** recommended career options align with how I prefer to work.

### US-09 — Define Financial and Career Constraints

**As a** user,  
**I want to** provide financial expectations, risk preferences and other constraints,  
**so that** recommendations consider my practical circumstances.

---

# Epic 2 — Career Fit & Options

### US-10 — Generate Career Fit Analysis

**As a** user,  
**I want CareerPilot AI to** analyze my experience, skills, goals and preferences,  
**so that** I can understand which career directions may fit me.

### US-11 — View Best-Fit Career Options

**As a** user,  
**I want to** receive a focused set of best-fit career options,  
**so that** I can explore realistic directions without being overwhelmed by too many choices.

### US-12 — View Alternative Career Options

**As a** user,  
**I want to** see additional probable career options,  
**so that** I can consider alternatives beyond the primary recommendations.

### US-13 — Understand Recommendation Rationale

**As a** user,  
**I want to** understand why each career option was recommended,  
**so that** I can evaluate whether the recommendation makes sense for me.

---

# Epic 3 — Compare & Decide

### US-14 — Compare Career Options

**As a** user,  
**I want to** compare career options using relevant criteria,  
**so that** I can understand their relative strengths and trade-offs.

### US-15 — Review Career Trade-offs

**As a** user,  
**I want to** review the potential benefits, challenges, fit factors and trade-offs of each option,  
**so that** I can make a more informed decision.

### US-16 — Select Preferred Career Direction

**As a** user,  
**I want to** identify my preferred career direction,  
**so that** I can move from exploration toward action.

---

# Epic 4 — Transition Plan & Refinement

### US-17 — Create Initial Transition Plan

**As a** user,  
**I want to** receive an initial transition plan for my selected career direction,  
**so that** I know what steps I can take next.

### US-18 — Refine Career Recommendation

**As a** user,  
**I want to** provide feedback and update my preferences,  
**so that** CareerPilot AI can refine future recommendations.

# MVP User Stories

The initial MVP prioritizes:

- US-01 to US-09 — Career Assessment & Profile
- US-10 — Career Fit Analysis
- US-11 — Best-Fit Career Options
- US-12 — Alternative Career Options
- US-13 — Recommendation Rationale
- US-14 — Compare Career Options
- US-16 — Preferred Career Direction
- US-17 — Initial Transition Plan

US-18 supports the refinement loop and can be expanded as the product evolves.
