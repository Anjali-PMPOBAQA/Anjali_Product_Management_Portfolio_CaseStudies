# CareerPilot AI — Product Epics

## 1. Epic Overview

The CareerPilot AI MVP is organized around four core product capabilities:

1. Career Assessment & Profile
2. Career Fit & Options
3. Compare & Decide
4. Transition Plan & Refinement

The epics translate the product strategy into deliverable product capabilities.

---

## 2. Epic 1 — Career Assessment & Profile

### Objective

Capture the user's professional background, skills, experience, goals, preferences and constraints to create a meaningful career profile.

### Features

- Professional Profile
- Skills & Experience Assessment
- Career Preferences & Priorities
- Financial & Work Constraints
- Assessment Summary

### User Outcome

The user has a structured profile representing their experience, capabilities, priorities and career constraints.

---

## 3. Epic 2 — Career Fit & Options

### Objective

Analyze the user's profile and identify career directions that align with their capabilities, goals and circumstances.

### Features

- Career Fit Analysis
- Transferable Skills Analysis
- Best-Fit Career Options
- Alternative Career Options
- Recommendation Rationale

### User Outcome

The user understands which career directions may fit their profile and why.

---

## 4. Epic 3 — Compare & Decide

### Objective

Help the user evaluate career options and move toward a clear career direction.

### Features

- Career Option Comparison
- Comparison Criteria
- Strengths & Trade-offs
- Preferred Career Direction
- Decision Confirmation

### User Outcome

The user can compare realistic options and identify a preferred career direction.

---

## 5. Epic 4 — Transition Plan & Refinement

### Objective

Convert the selected career direction into an actionable starting plan and allow the recommendation to improve through user feedback.

### Features

- Initial Transition Plan
- Recommended Next Steps
- Skill Gaps & Learning Areas
- Feedback & Refinement
- Updated Recommendations

### User Outcome

The user leaves the experience with an actionable next step and the ability to refine the direction over time.

---

## 6. Epic-to-Outcome Map

| Epic | Primary User Outcome |
|---|---|
| Career Assessment & Profile | Understand and structure current capabilities and priorities |
| Career Fit & Options | Discover relevant career directions |
| Compare & Decide | Evaluate options and choose a direction |
| Transition Plan & Refinement | Turn the decision into actionable next steps |

---

## 7. MVP Delivery Boundary

### MVP

- Career Assessment & Profile
- Career Fit Analysis
- Career Options
- Option Comparison
- Preferred Career Direction
- Initial Transition Plan

### Later Releases

- Deeper personalization
- Recommendation refinement
- Advanced integrations
- Community features
- Automated job applications
- Deeper financial modelling

The MVP focuses on helping users move from **career uncertainty → career clarity → actionable next step**.
