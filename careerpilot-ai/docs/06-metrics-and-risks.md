# 06 — Metrics, Risks & Assumptions

## 1. Product Outcome

The primary product outcome is to move users from career uncertainty to a clear career direction and an actionable transition plan.

## 2. Core Product Funnel

Assessment → Recommendation → Comparison → Decision → Plan

## 3. Success Metrics

### Assessment Completion

Measures the percentage of users who complete the assessment.

**Why it matters:**  
Indicates whether the onboarding and assessment experience provides enough value for users to continue.

### Activation

Measures users who complete the initial assessment and reach a meaningful product outcome, such as viewing their career-fit or recommendation results.

**Why it matters:**  
Shows whether users reach the core value of the product.

### Recommendation Engagement

Measures interaction with recommended career options, such as viewing, exploring or selecting options.

**Why it matters:**  
Indicates whether recommendations are relevant enough to support career exploration.

### Plan Creation

Measures users who create or reach an initial transition plan.

**Why it matters:**  
Shows whether the product moves beyond exploration toward action.

### Recommendation Refinement

Measures users who provide feedback or modify preferences or recommendations.

**Why it matters:**  
Indicates whether the refinement loop is being used to improve personalization.

### User Satisfaction

Measures user-reported satisfaction with the experience.

**Why it matters:**  
Provides a qualitative signal of perceived usefulness and confidence.
