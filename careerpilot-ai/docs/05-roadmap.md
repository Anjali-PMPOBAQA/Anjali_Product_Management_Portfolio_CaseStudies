# 05 — Product Roadmap

## 1. Roadmap Objective

The CareerPilot AI roadmap is designed to evolve the product from a focused career-clarity MVP into a more personalized and intelligent career-transition experience.

The roadmap follows the principle:

> Validate the core career decision journey first, then expand personalization and intelligence.

---

## 2. Roadmap Strategy

The product evolves through four stages:

**MVP → Personalization → Refinement → Intelligent Experience**

Each release builds on the previous product capability rather than introducing unrelated functionality.

---

# 3. Release 1.0 — MVP

### Theme

**Career Clarity & Decision Support**

### Objective

Validate the core journey from career assessment to actionable career direction.

### Key Capabilities

- Professional profile
- Skills and experience assessment
- Career preferences and priorities
- Career fit analysis
- Career options
- Option comparison
- Preferred career direction
- Initial transition plan
