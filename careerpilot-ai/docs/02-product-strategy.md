# 02 — Product Strategy

## 1. Product Vision

CareerPilot AI helps experienced professionals make informed career-transition decisions by connecting their existing capabilities, personal priorities and future opportunities into a clear, personalized and actionable career path.

---

## 2. Product Goal

Move users from:

**"I don't know what to do next."**

to:

**"I understand my options and know what to do next."**

The product focuses on helping users reach a clear career direction rather than simply providing more career information.

---

## 3. Product Principles

### 1. Guided
Lead users through a structured decision journey instead of presenting a large amount of information at once.

### 2. Personalized
Recommendations should consider the user's experience, skills, goals, preferences and constraints.

### 3. Transparent
Users should understand why a career option is being suggested and what factors influence the recommendation.

### 4. Action-Oriented
The experience should move beyond recommendations and help users identify practical next steps.

### 5. Flexible
Users should be able to review and refine their information as their understanding evolves.

### 6. Low Cognitive Load
Keep the number of choices manageable and present information in a way that supports decision-making.

---

## 4. Product Strategy

The product strategy is built around a simple progression:

**Assess → Analyze → Discover → Compare → Decide → Plan → Refine**

### Assess
Understand the user's professional background, skills, experience, goals, work preferences and constraints.

### Analyze
Identify relevant capabilities and transferable skills and evaluate career-fit signals.

### Discover
Present a focused set of potential career directions.

### Compare
Help the user understand the differences and trade-offs between options.

### Decide
Allow the user to identify a preferred career direction.

### Plan
Translate the selected direction into an initial transition plan.

### Refine
Use user feedback and updated preferences to improve the experience over time.

---

## 5. MVP Hypothesis

> Can personalized career guidance help experienced professionals move from career uncertainty toward a clear and actionable career direction?

The MVP is designed to test this core product hypothesis before expanding into more advanced capabilities.

---

## 6. MVP Scope

### Included in MVP

#### 1. User Profile & Assessment
Capture:

- Professional experience
- Current role and industry
- Skills
- Skill proficiency
- Transferable skills
- Career goals
- Work preferences
- Financial and practical constraints

#### 2. Career Fit Analysis
Analyze the user's profile and identify capabilities and factors relevant to potential career directions.

#### 3. Career Options
Present:

- 3 best-fit career options
- 5 probable alternative options

The focused output is intended to support exploration without overwhelming the user.

#### 4. Option Comparison
Allow users to compare career options against relevant factors such as:

- Career fit
- Growth
- Flexibility
- User priorities
- Constraints

#### 5. Preferred Career Direction
Allow the user to identify the career direction they want to explore further.

#### 6. Initial Transition Plan
Provide practical next steps for beginning the transition.

#### 7. Feedback & Refinement
Allow users to review and refine their preferences and recommendations.

---

## 7. MVP Boundary

### In Scope

The MVP focuses on:

**Career assessment → Personalized options → Comparison → Decision → Initial plan**

### Deferred

The following capabilities are intentionally deferred:

- Advanced personalization
- Extensive external integrations
- Community features
- Automated job applications
- Deep financial modelling

These capabilities can be evaluated after the core career-clarity experience is validated.

---

## 8. Value Proposition

CareerPilot AI provides a structured career decision experience that connects:

**Experience + Skills + Goals + Preferences + Constraints**

to:

**Relevant Career Options + Comparison + Decision + Action Plan**

The product is designed to help users make a career decision rather than simply consume more career information.

---

## 9. Product Lifecycle

The product lifecycle is structured as:

**Discovery**
↓
Understand the problem, users and opportunity

**Definition**
↓
Define the product vision, MVP and requirements

**UX & Prototype**
↓
Translate requirements into user journeys, screens and prototypes

**Backlog**
↓
Convert product requirements into epics, features, user stories and acceptance criteria

**Development**
↓
Build prioritized capabilities in Agile sprints

**QA Validation**
↓
Validate functionality against user stories and acceptance criteria

**Staging**
↓
Validate the release in a staging environment

**Release**
↓
Deliver the prioritized product increment

**Measurement**
↓
Evaluate product metrics and user feedback

**Refinement**
↓
Improve recommendations, experience and future roadmap

---

## 10. Strategic Product Decision

The key strategic decision for the MVP is to focus on **career clarity and decision support**.

Instead of attempting to become a complete career ecosystem immediately, CareerPilot AI first aims to help users answer three questions:

1. **What career options fit me?**
2. **Which option should I explore further?**
3. **What should I do next?**

This creates a focused foundation for future personalization and intelligent career-transition capabilities.
