# 01 — Product Discovery

## 1. Discovery Objective

CareerPilot AI was conceived to help experienced professionals move from career uncertainty to a clear, realistic and actionable career direction.

The discovery focus was to understand:

- What makes career transition decisions difficult for experienced professionals
- What information users need before choosing a direction
- How existing skills and experience can be connected to future career opportunities
- How to reduce information overload and decision complexity

---

## 2. Problem Context

Experienced professionals with 5–20 years of experience may reach a point where their current career no longer provides the growth, flexibility or alignment they are looking for.

The challenge is not necessarily a lack of career information.

There are already many sources of:

- Job opportunities
- Career lists
- Online courses
- Career advice
- AI tools
- Skill assessments

The larger problem is turning this information into a **personalized career decision**.

Users need to understand:

> Which career paths realistically fit my experience, skills, priorities and circumstances — and what should I do next?

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Experienced professionals considering a career transition need a structured way to connect their existing capabilities, personal priorities and constraints with realistic future career options.

Without this structure, users may explore too many possibilities without reaching a clear decision or actionable next step.

---

## 4. Target Users

CareerPilot AI focuses on experienced professionals who are:

- Considering a career change
- Looking for better growth opportunities
- Seeking greater flexibility or work-life alignment
- Returning after a career break
- Exploring alternative career paths
- Considering business or independent career options

### Primary Experience Range

Approximately 5–20 years of professional experience.

---

## 5. Primary Persona — Sonali

**Name:** Sonali  
**Experience:** 15 years in IT/software development

### Situation

Sonali has significant professional experience but feels that her current career has limited growth and does not provide the flexibility and sustainability she wants.

She is exploring different possibilities but is unsure which direction is realistically suitable for her.

### Goals

- Find meaningful career growth
- Explore alternative career directions
- Achieve greater flexibility
- Build a sustainable career
- Make a practical and informed decision

### Current Behaviour

Sonali already uses:

- ChatGPT
- Online courses
- Career lists
- Internet research

However, she struggles to convert this information into a clear decision and actionable plan.

### Core Frustration

There are many possible choices, but no clear way to determine which options best fit her experience, priorities and circumstances.

### Desired Outcome

A clear understanding of:

1. Which career options fit her
2. Why they fit
3. How the options compare
4. Which direction she could choose
5. What she should do next

---

## 6. User Need

> "Help me understand which career paths realistically fit my experience, goals and circumstances — and what I should do next."

This became the central user need behind CareerPilot AI.

---

## 7. Jobs To Be Done

### Functional Job

When I am uncertain about my next career direction, I want to evaluate my experience, skills, priorities and constraints against realistic career options, so that I can choose a direction and understand my next steps.

### Emotional Job

I want to feel less overwhelmed and more confident about my career decision.

### Outcome

Move from:

**"I don't know what to do next."**

to:

**"I understand my options and know what to do next."**

---

## 8. Opportunity

The opportunity is to bring several pieces of career decision-making together in one guided experience:

**Existing Capabilities**

Skills + Experience + Transferable Skills

↓

**Personal Priorities**

Goals + Work Style + Preferences

↓

**Constraints**

Location + Income Expectations + Risk + Work Arrangement

↓

**Career Opportunities**

Relevant Career Paths

↓

**Decision Support**

Compare → Decide → Plan

CareerPilot AI therefore focuses on **reducing decision complexity**, rather than simply providing more career information.

---

## 9. Discovery Hypotheses

The following hypotheses shaped the initial product direction and would require validation through real user research and product usage:

### Hypothesis 1
Experienced professionals need personalized career guidance rather than generic lists of possible careers.

### Hypothesis 2
Users may benefit from evaluating career options against their existing skills, experience and personal priorities.

### Hypothesis 3
Presenting a focused set of relevant options may reduce decision overload compared with presenting a large list of possibilities.

### Hypothesis 4
Comparing career options before making a decision can help users understand trade-offs between different paths.

### Hypothesis 5
A transition plan can help convert career decisions into actionable next steps.

---

## 10. Discovery Conclusion

The initial product direction was to solve **career decision complexity** first.

CareerPilot AI therefore starts with the user's professional background, skills, goals, preferences and constraints before presenting career options.

The intended experience is:

**Assess → Analyze → Discover → Compare → Decide → Plan → Refine**

This discovery direction became the foundation for the product strategy and MVP scope.
